abstract: >-
  A physics-informed harmonization framework for lung CT was evaluated across multiple imaging tasks, including lung structure segmentation, emphysema quantification, longitudinal emphysema assessment, and lung nodule quantification. Results demonstrate improved consistency across scanner and reconstruction variability, supporting more generalizable AI and quantitative imaging analyses.
