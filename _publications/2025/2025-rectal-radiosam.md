pub:            "Physics and Imaging in Radiation Oncology"

abstract: >-
  Rectal-RadioSAM combines four fine-tuned MedSAM networks with radiomic feature extraction and XGBoost to predict pathological complete response in rectal cancer. It uses MRI before and after neoadjuvant chemoradiotherapy for automated tumor segmentation and treatment-response assessment.
